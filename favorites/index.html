What follows is my own experience with Gomi, rather than the seller's description or a promise that every dog will have the same experience.

The dog leash is here because I like this brand enough to keep using it. It extends and retracts with plenty of room for Gomi to move, and it is lightweight and comfortable to use. Even with those moving parts, both leashes have held up very well and feel sturdy in everyday use. One leash has been with us for more than seven years and the other for more than five, without a problem with either. I bought the second for a different color, not because the first broke, and I still alternate between them. These are two overlapping ownership periods, not a twelve-year test of a single leash.

The dog toy earns its place in a simpler way: when I ask Gomi to bring a toy, this is the one she brings first. Its length also makes it comfortable for us to play tug together. The Flying Ring is her outdoor favorite. She loves going after it; the family video below shows her chasing and bringing back her flying disc in 2019. It has lasted well in our use, even when she mouths and chews it, without easily breaking apart. That is how it has held up with Gomi, not a claim that it is indestructible or suitable for every dog's chewing style.

The eye products belong to a different part of our day. We use Gentle Eye Wash daily to clean away debris as part of Gomi's veterinarian-guided care. With the wash we used before, she would turn her head away; with this one, she stays still and does not seem bothered during cleaning. That difference is why I like it.

With Soothing Eye Lube, the difference I notice is the amount of discharge the next morning. On occasions when I forgot it for about a day, there was much more eye discharge the following morning. When we use it as directed, I usually find very little. I am describing what I see with Gomi, not a controlled test or evidence that it treats the cause of an eye problem. Missing a day is not an experiment I recommend.

Gomi's eye treatment and schedule come from her veterinarian. Depending on her condition, we go back roughly every two weeks, and sooner if my daily checks for bleeding or changes in discharge raise a concern. Ask your own vet which eye products are appropriate, and do not change or skip prescribed care to try these. Our eye-care story and observation prompts explain the routine in more detail.
